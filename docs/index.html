QuranPause

وقفة قرآن

An iPhone app for making time for the Quran: lock the apps that distract you until you have read or listened, with prayer times, azan, and the Qibla alongside.

  • The printed 604-page Madinah Mushaf, drawn with the King Fahd Complex's own page fonts. Tap any word to select that ayah, then copy it or listen to it.
  • Prayer times and azan alerts for your location, calculated offline, with six calculation methods.
  • Qibla bearing from true north, with a live compass where the device supports one.
  • Read or listen to earn your time. Ten verified reciters, Lock Screen controls, English and Arabic throughout.
  • No account, no server, no analytics.
